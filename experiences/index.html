    Winter sports

    Skiing, snowboarding & snow tubing

    Lake Harmony offers a double dose of winter fun with two major ski areas in one central location! Jack Frost and Big Boulder Ski Areas are top destinations for skiing, riding, tubing and terrain park thrills. The mountains host regular races, competitions and family-friendly events throughout the winter.
