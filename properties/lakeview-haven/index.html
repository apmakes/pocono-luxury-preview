The home

A haven for gatherings and feasts

Unveil the brand-new Luxurious Lakeview Haven: a 9-bedroom, 7.5-bath retreat beyond compare. Revel in tranquil relaxation and wholesome recreation, with endless amenities for all. Whether you’re embarking on a skiing escapade or crafting precious memories at a family reunion, every aspect is perfected. Enveloped by nature’s beauty, your ultimate getaway awaits.

Nestled at Lake Harmony’s edge, Paddles and Poles Haven offers the ultimate lakeview retreat. With 4,700 sq ft of living space, it’s perfect for large families, multiple groups, or friend getaways. Our philosophy: “Whatever Floats Your Boat!”

A haven for gatherings and feasts. Fully-equipped kitchen with stainless steel appliances, quartz countertops, breakfast bar, and essentials. The expansive dining table seats 25+, while the cozy living area, 55” TV, and gas fireplace provide relaxation. Step onto the deck for BBQs, alfresco dining, and lake sunsets.
